# Queue

<Row>
  <Phone src="/img/help/1.7/09_now_playing.webp" alt="Now playing queue" />
  <Phone src="/img/help/1.6/28_queue_context_menu.webp" alt="Track actions" />
</Row>

The **Queue** screen (accessible from the drawer) shows MusicBee's active playing queue. It opens on the track that is playing, with a couple of tracks above it for context. If you start scrolling before the playing track is found, the queue stays where you put it.

- **Playing indicator**: Highlights the currently playing track
- **Jump to playing track**: The target button in the top bar scrolls back to the playing track after you have scrolled away
- **Queue size**: Displayed in the header
- **Search**: Find a track in the queue
- **Swipe right**: Remove a track from the queue
- **Long-press and drag**: Reorder tracks
- **Pull down**: Refresh the queue

The queue refreshes automatically when you navigate to the screen.

## Track Actions

Long-press a track in the queue to access its context menu:

- **Go to Album**: Navigate to the track's album in the library
- **Go to Artist**: Navigate to the track's artist in the library
