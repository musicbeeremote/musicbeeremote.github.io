[v1.7.1](https://github.com/musicbeeremote/mbrc/releases/tag/v1.7.1) is out on the open testing channel today, going to everyone on it at once. It stays there for a week, then moves to the Play Store as a staged rollout: 20% of users in the second week, 50% in the third, and everyone after that. It is mostly a fix release for what came up after v1.7.0, plus a new language and the app side of connecting by QR code.

---

## Simplified Chinese

The app now speaks Simplified Chinese, thanks to yangf73, who translated it. It follows your phone's language, and on Android 13 and later you can also pick it for MusicBee Remote alone in the system's per-app language setting. Anything not translated yet falls back to English.

If you would like to see the app in your language, [TRANSLATING.md](https://github.com/musicbeeremote/mbrc/blob/main/TRANSLATING.md) explains how to send a translation.

## Getting ready for QR code connect

The next major plugin release, 2.0, will show a QR code in its setup and settings. Scan it with your phone's camera and the app opens, shows the computer's name and addresses, and asks before connecting. It tries every address at once and saves the first one that answers as your default connection, so there is nothing to type.

This helps exactly where scanning the network does not: guest networks, mesh Wi-Fi that blocks discovery, or a computer on another subnet. Only addresses on your local network are accepted.

Current plugin versions do not show a QR code, so nothing changes for you until plugin 2.0 is out. v1.7.1 just makes sure the app is ready when it is.

## Fixes worth calling out

- **Swiping the app away from recents closes it again.** Since v1.7.0, doing that during playback left the app running, with its notification stuck in place and the volume keys still moving MusicBee's volume. Now swiping it away always closes it. MusicBee keeps playing, and opening the app again reconnects.
- **The navigation drawer no longer stays open on tablets and in landscape.** v1.7.0 kept it pinned down the side on wide screens, took space from the player and the library, and removed the menu button. It is back to opening from the menu button at every screen size. Pressing back while it is open now closes it instead of leaving the app, and with gesture navigation, swiping in from the lower middle of the left edge opens it.
- **The elapsed time kept freezing after a reconnect.** If the connection dropped and came back while MusicBee kept playing, the time only moved when MusicBee sent an update. It now counts every second again.
- **The media controls in the notification shade stopped updating every second.** The constant updates kept pushing the remote ahead of your phone's own player and used battery for nothing. The notification now only changes when the track, the playing state or the cover changes, and the progress bar moves on its own in between.
- **Two phones kept knocking each other off MusicBee** when one was set up from the other's backup or with a phone transfer tool such as Smart Switch. Both carried the same identity, so the plugin dropped one whenever the other connected. That identity is no longer backed up or transferred, and every phone picks a new one after this update, so you do not need to clear app data.
- **Streams show a red dot and "LIVE"** where the player and the lyrics screen show the track length, instead of repeating the elapsed time.
- **Three crashes are gone**: scrolling a library list while the library was syncing, opening Help on devices where Android System WebView fails to load (Help now offers to open the page in your browser), and connecting to a port where some other program was listening instead of the plugin.

The [full changelog](https://github.com/musicbeeremote/mbrc/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) has the details. v1.7.1 works with the plugin you already have, and [plugin v1.5.0](/news/2026-08-31-plugin-v1-5-0) is still the one to get.

## Getting it

If you are on the open testing channel, v1.7.1 is already on its way. If not, the Play Store brings it to you sometime in the next three to four weeks. To get it sooner, [join the test channel](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kelsos.mbrc), or download the Play APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/musicbeeremote/mbrc/releases/tag/v1.7.1) and install it. It is the same build as on the Play Store, so the Play Store keeps it updated from there.

There is also a separate GitHub APK in the release if you would rather avoid Crashlytics and Firebase. That one you update by hand.

## What's next

v1.8.0 picks up the plugin's network discovery, adds a fast scroller to the library lists, and brings back one volume step per key press instead of ten.
